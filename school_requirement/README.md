## Audited requirements

- The registered project title is **Nano-Mamba U-Net: A Spatio-Temporal Framework for Cardiac Motion Analysis** and must remain unchanged in the thesis and presentation.
- The P2 report contributes 40% and the P2 presentation contributes 20% of the project assessment described in the supplied briefing.
- The final report is expected to include the material from Chapters 1–5 together with Chapter 6 (Results), Chapter 7 (Discussion and Limitations), Chapter 8 (Conclusion and Future Work), and the reference list.
- The final presentation is 15 minutes, followed by a 15-minute question-and-answer session.
- The final submission should use the applicable university/project template and demonstrate the technical work completed during the project.
